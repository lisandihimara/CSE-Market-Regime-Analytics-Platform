# CSE Market Regime Analytics Platform 📈

An AI-powered financial analytics platform designed to identify, analyze, and visualize market regimes in the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE). The platform applies data science, statistical analysis, and machine learning techniques to understand changing market conditions and provide data-driven insights for investors and analysts.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Financial markets continuously change between different conditions such as bullish trends, bearish declines, high volatility periods, and stable market phases. Identifying these market regimes can help investors, analysts, and financial institutions better understand market behavior and make informed decisions.

The **CSE Market Regime Analytics Platform** focuses on detecting historical market patterns and classifying market conditions using quantitative techniques.

The project transforms raw market data into meaningful insights through:

- Data preprocessing and feature engineering
- Exploratory data analysis
- Market trend and volatility analysis
- Regime detection techniques
- Machine learning-based classification
- Interactive visualization of market behavior

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze historical Colombo Stock Exchange market data
- Identify different market states and regime changes
- Study relationships between market trends, volatility, and returns
- Develop analytical models to detect market conditions
- Provide visual insights through an interactive analytics platform

---

## 🏦 Business Problem

Traditional market analysis often relies on manual interpretation of charts and historical trends. However, financial markets are complex systems influenced by changing volatility, investor sentiment, and economic conditions.

This project addresses the problem:

> "How can data-driven techniques be used to identify different market regimes and provide meaningful insights into stock market behavior?"
